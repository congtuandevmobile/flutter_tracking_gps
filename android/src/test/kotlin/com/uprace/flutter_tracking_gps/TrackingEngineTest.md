# TrackingEngineTest.kt

Engine tests with a fake location source, a fake step source and an in-memory journal store: fixes become distance; the journal holds a header and every fix in order; `lastAcceptedFixAtMs` is calendar time; pause/resume never bridge; a location arriving after pause is dropped by the generation gate; `stop` flushes the journal and returns a final result; restore replays the journal and comes back paused with the same result, using the header's configuration; a half-written last record is dropped and the journal repaired; nothing to restore throws; a session cannot be cleared while open and clearing deletes the journal; sessions are listed; persisted fixes keep their original times; a wall-clock jump does not age fixes; a failed write is reported while recording continues; a missing permission becomes a state and an error; adaptive sampling re-requests; steps in a degraded interval become a pending estimate (not distance); pause/resume register and unregister the sensor; a missing step permission or steps off never block recording; steps walked while paused are not counted; restored steps and stride come back from the journal.

Added: `stop` is FINISHING while the journal is flushed and only then STOPPED; the snapshot reports events not on disk; damage in the middle keeps a backup, a truncated tail needs none; a journal of another engine version is restored but flagged.
