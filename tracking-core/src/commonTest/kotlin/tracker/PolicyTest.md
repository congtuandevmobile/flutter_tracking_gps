# PolicyTest.kt

`TimeModelTest` (active time excludes pauses; a weak start, a gap open at finish and a gap beyond `maxBridge` are reported as uncredited; a bridged gap counts as moving time), `StepEvidenceTest` (a long gap with no steps is standing still; with steps it is bridged; without a working sensor, in a short gap, or for cycling silence is not evidence), `StepIntervalTest` (a batch wholly in good GPS never becomes pending; wholly in a weak interval it does; straddling a quality change it is ambiguous; delivered after GPS came back it is too late; a batch of an earlier leg is refused), `StationaryPolicyTest` (jitter after a stop is not distance and real movement is credited in full; unknown speed falls back to the displacement speed), `TrustedPdrPolicyTest` (a calibrated stride credits a long gap as estimated distance; the conservative policy credits nothing; a configured stride alone is not calibration). The Dart twin is `test/engine/policy_test.dart`.
